Selected research & projects

01

Do Seawalls Pay for Themselves?

Environmental economics · Two-way fixed effects · Stata · 2026 working paper

Examines how coastal risk relates to homeowners' insurance premiums in South Florida. Uses a county-quarter panel framework and connects estimated insurance-risk relationships to a net-present-value break-even calculation for protective infrastructure.

02

Soda Tax: Demand Elasticity & Revenue Simulation

Applied microeconomics · Log-log demand model · Excel Power Query · Scenario analysis

Models an illustrative 5% Virginia soda tax using simulated store-level sales and ZIP-level income data. Estimates price elasticities by income group (approximately −0.98 overall in the simulated data) and tests demand and revenue sensitivity to tax pass-through assumptions.

03

Green Funding and Carbon Dynamics in South Africa

Time-series econometrics · R · ARIMA · VAR · Johansen cointegration

Analyzes monthly macroeconomic and environmental series using unit-root tests, ARIMA, VAR, and Johansen cointegration methods. The academic exercise identifies two cointegrating relationships and explores short-run dynamics through impulse responses and forecast-error variance decomposition.
